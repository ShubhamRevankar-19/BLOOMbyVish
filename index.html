    Our story

    More Than Flowers.

    BLOOM by Vish creates handmade pipe-cleaner flowers designed to turn creativity into meaningful gifts.

    It began with a simple belief: the best gifts are the ones that carry a little piece of the person who made them. So every bouquet here is shaped slowly and personally — bent, twisted and layered until it feels like it was meant for one person only.

    There are no factories and no shortcuts. Just colour, patience, a steady hand, and the quiet joy of watching a petal become a bloom.

    The handmade process

    Every petal is shaped by hand, so no two blooms are ever identical.

    Our philosophy

    Gifts should feel personal. We make flowers that say what words can't.

    A small business

    Made in small batches, packed by hand, and finished with care.
